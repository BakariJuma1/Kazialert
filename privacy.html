Kazi Alert

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 June 2026  ·  Built by Isaac Bakari Juma, Jaribu Tech Solutions

The short version

Kazi Alert is a personal job-hunting tool. Your CV and personal data never reach our servers — we don't have any. The extension stores everything locally on your device and connects only to the third-party APIs you configure yourself (Groq and Resend), using your own API keys.

What data is used and where it goes

Local only CV text
Your CV is stored in chrome.storage.local — on your device only, never uploaded to any server controlled by Kazi Alert. It is sent to Groq's API solely to compute a match score against each job listing, on demand, using your own Groq API key.
Sent to: Groq, Inc. — subject to their privacy policy. You can delete your CV at any time from Options → Your CV → Remove.
Local only Alert email address
Your email address is stored locally and passed to Resend solely to deliver job alert emails, using your own Resend API key.
Sent to: Resend, Inc. — subject to their privacy policy.
Your own service Applied jobs (Job Tracker)
When you click "Mark Applied" or use the manual log form, the job title, company name, and date are written to a Google Sheet via a Google Apps Script you deploy yourself. This script runs under your own Google account — Kazi Alert never has access to your sheet.
Sent to: Your own Google Apps Script endpoint, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
Local only Scan logs, match history, seen jobs
Scan activity logs, your match history, and the list of already-seen job IDs are stored only in chrome.storage.local. They are never transmitted anywhere.
Never collected Browsing history & page content
Content scripts run on job pages you visit to passively detect job listings. They read only job-related fields (title, company, description, apply link) from the current page. No browsing history, form data, passwords, or unrelated page content is ever read or stored.

API keys

Kazi Alert uses a Bring Your Own Key model. You create your own accounts with Groq and Resend, generate API keys, and paste them into the extension. Your keys are stored in chrome.storage.local and are never transmitted to or stored by the developer of this extension.

Keep your API keys private. If you believe a key has been compromised, revoke it immediately in the respective provider's dashboard.

Permissions used and why

  • storage — save your CV, settings, and scan history locally
  • alarms — run scheduled scans at the interval you choose
  • tabs & scripting — inject a passive content script to read job listings on pages you visit
  • notifications — show a system notification when matches are found
  • offscreen — create a hidden document with DOM access to parse HTML/RSS job feeds in the background
  • host_permissions (all URLs) — fetch job listings from the many different job board and company career page URLs you configure, including custom URLs you add yourself. No page content is stored or transmitted beyond what is needed for matching.

Data retention and deletion

All data is stored in your browser's local storage. You can delete any or all of it at any time:

  • CV — Options → Your CV → Remove
  • Match history — Options → Match History → Clear all
  • Scan logs — Options → Scan Logs → Clear
  • All data — Uninstall the extension. Chrome automatically deletes all chrome.storage.local data for uninstalled extensions.

Children's privacy

Kazi Alert is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and update the extension in the Chrome Web Store. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
